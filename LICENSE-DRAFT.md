## 1. Purpose and interpretation

TSL is intended to be a copyleft software license. It is designed to allow use, study, modification, and redistribution while preserving attribution, requiring source availability for distributed covered works, and preventing recipients from using intellectual-property claims to take control of or obstruct the covered project.

This draft must be interpreted according to its actual wording and applicable law. It does not create rights that the Licensor or a Contributor does not legally hold or have authority to grant.

- **Contribution** means copyrightable material or a patentable invention intentionally submitted for inclusion in the Project.

- **Source Code** means the preferred form of a work for making modifications.
- **Object Code** means a compiled or otherwise machine-readable form that is not the preferred form for modification.
- **Distribution** means conveying a copy of a Covered Work to another person or entity. Merely running a copy without conveying it is not distribution.
- **Necessary Patent Claims** means patent claims owned or controlled by a party that would be infringed by exercising permissions expressly granted for that party's contribution or covered material, excluding claims infringed only because of further modifications or combinations not supplied by that party.
